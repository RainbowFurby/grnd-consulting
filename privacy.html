Privacy Notice

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · GRND Consulting, Malaysia

This notice explains what personal data GRND Consulting ("I", "me") collects through this website, why, and what rights you have over it. It's written to be consistent with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

What I collect

When you use the contact form on this site, I collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Whatever you write in the message field

If you message me on WhatsApp instead, I receive whatever your WhatsApp profile and conversation share with me directly — that exchange is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, since it's operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.

Why I collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry — to understand what you're asking about and get back to you by email or WhatsApp. I don't use form submissions for marketing, and I don't sell or rent your data to anyone.

How your data is handled

The contact form is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form-relay service that forwards your submission to my inbox by email. It does not appear anywhere else on this site, and I don't run analytics or advertising trackers that build a profile of visitors.

How long I keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and, if we work together, for the duration of that engagement plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping. You can ask me to delete your data at any time (see below).

Your rights under the PDPA

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data I hold about you
  • Ask me to correct inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent and ask me to delete your data, where I'm not required to keep it for legal or contractual reasons

To exercise any of these, email notification@staygrnd.xyz and I'll respond within a reasonable time.

Security

I take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect the data you share, including relying on reputable third-party processors (Web3Forms, WhatsApp/Meta) that maintain their own security standards. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so this is a best-effort commitment, not a guarantee.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your data — email notification@staygrnd.xyz or WhatsApp me directly.
